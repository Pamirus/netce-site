Netçe Privacy Policy and KVKK Notice

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information Netçe keeps, where it is kept, and how personal data is processed when you use the app. It is also the privacy notice required by Article 10 of Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The Turkish version is the binding one; this is a translation.

In short

  • You can use Netçe without an account. I never ask for your name, email address, phone number, age or school.
  • Unless you sign in, your study history, subjects, nickname, reminder times and settings stay on your phone only. They are never sent to me or to any server, and I cannot see them.
  • If you like, you can sign in with Apple or Google to back up your study history. Your sessions and study setup are then stored with your account on Supabase's servers in Frankfurt (Germany, EU). With Apple I do not receive your name or email; with Google, Google shares your email address, name and a link to your profile photo. You can delete your account in the app at any time.
  • Netçe has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. I do not profile anyone, use data for advertising, or sell it.
  • If you have not signed in, the app goes online only for purchases: it contacts a service called RevenueCat to check your Pro status. Apple or Google takes the payment; I never see your card details.
  • Your study card is made on your phone and leaves it only if you share it.
  • You can delete all Netçe data on your phone with "Delete all my data" in Settings.

If you do, your study history and study setup are backed up to your account and come back when you change phones or move between iPhone and Android.

I use a service called Supabase for accounts and backups. If you sign in, the following is processed:

4. Your study card

The card is made on your phone as an image. It shows the day's (or week's) study time, the split by subject, your streak, the days left to your exam, your nickname if you set one, and Netçe's name. You see what is on the card before you share it.

The card leaves your phone only when you tap share and pick an app in your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes. A card you send to apps like Instagram or WhatsApp is subject to those services' own rules. Nothing about this sharing reaches me.

You don't have to use your real name as your nickname. If you share your card publicly, I suggest you don't.

5. Purchases: RevenueCat, Apple and Google

You buy Netçe Pro through the App Store or Google Play. The payment screen is Apple's or Google's own: your card details, name, Apple ID or Google account never reach me.

I use a service called RevenueCat to validate purchases and turn Pro on. In versions where purchases are enabled, the app contacts RevenueCat when it opens and when it comes back to the foreground, to check your Pro status. So the following is processed even if you never buy anything:

  • A random anonymous ID generated on your phone by the RevenueCat library. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or your device's advertising ID.
  • Device type, operating system and app version, language, store country and currency, and when you last used the app.
  • If you bought something, the receipt or purchase token issued by the store. It shows which product you bought, when and at what price, trial and renewal dates, and the store's transaction ID.
  • As with any internet connection, your IP address reaches RevenueCat's servers.

In RevenueCat's dashboard I see purchase records under this anonymous ID (product, date, price, country); I cannot see who you are.

Apple and Google process the payment and your store account under their own privacy policies, as separate data controllers. They tell me, through RevenueCat, that a purchase happened and whether it is still valid.

6. App Store and Google Play statistics

Apple and Google show developers aggregate statistics such as downloads, countries and crash reports. Apple and Google collect these themselves, depending on the "share with app developers" or "usage and diagnostics" setting on your phone. Netçe has no separate analytics library of its own for this.

RevenueCat processes it on my behalf under our data processing agreement, not for its own purposes. RevenueCat privacy policy

  • Apple and Google: they process the payment and your store account on their own behalf, as separate data controllers. If you sign in with them, they verify the sign-in with your account and send me the information in section 3. I do not transfer personal data to them. Apple privacy policy · Google privacy policy
  • Competent public authorities: only when the law requires it, and only to the extent required.

I do not share information with anyone else, and I never sell it. The service providers that process data on my behalf (RevenueCat and Supabase) are bound by our agreements to provide the same or equal protection as this policy.

Transfer abroad: Because RevenueCat's servers are in the USA, the information in section 5 is transferred abroad. The Turkish Personal Data Protection Board has no adequacy decision for the USA. The transfer relies on one of the appropriate safeguards listed in KVKK Art. 9: the standard contract published by the Board, signed with RevenueCat and notified to the Board within 5 business days of signing.

If you sign in, the information in section 3 is also transferred abroad, to Supabase's servers in Germany. The Board has no adequacy decision for Germany either. Supabase is a US company, so the information may also be accessed from other countries for support and maintenance. These transfers rely on the same safeguard: the Board's standard contract, signed with Supabase and notified to the Board within 5 business days.

10. How long it is kept

  • Information on your phone: until you delete it with "Delete all my data" or remove the app.
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat: as long as the purchase is valid, so that Pro works and can be restored. After that, only as long as legal retention duties (for example tax and consumer law) require; then they are deleted or anonymized.
  • RevenueCat records without a purchase: these hold only the anonymous ID, device information and last-used time. When you remove the app, the ID is deleted from your phone too and the record can no longer be matched to anyone. I delete these records if you ask, or when I stop using RevenueCat.
  • Your account and backup (Supabase): as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your account, sessions and setup are deleted from the server at once; copies in Supabase's technical logs and backups are deleted when their own retention period ends.
  • Emails: until the matter is closed; then deleted unless the law requires me to keep them.

11. Deleting your data

  • From the phone: Settings → Privacy → "Delete all my data" deletes your study history, subjects, nickname, reminders, running timer and other settings from the phone, and setup starts over. If you signed in, you are also signed out, but the backup in your account is not deleted. Your Pro purchase is not affected, since it is a purchase.
  • Your account and backup: Settings → Account and backup → "Delete account" permanently deletes your account and all your sessions and setup on the server. It then asks whether the data on your phone should stay or be deleted too.
  • By removing the app: if you remove Netçe from your phone, all Netçe information on the phone is deleted, including the Pro status and the anonymous RevenueCat ID. Your account and backup, if you have one, stay until you delete them.
  • For records at RevenueCat: write to me (section 13).

Deletion cannot be undone. If you deleted your account or never signed in, I cannot bring back deleted study history either.

12. Your rights under KVKK Article 11

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you can ask me to:

  • a) tell you whether your personal data is processed,
  • b) give you information about it if it is,
  • c) tell you the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose,
  • ç) tell you the third parties, in Turkey or abroad, it is transferred to,
  • d) correct it if it is incomplete or wrong,
  • e) delete or destroy it under the conditions of KVKK Article 7,
  • f) notify the third parties it was transferred to of the steps taken under (d) and (e),
  • g) consider your objection to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis of your data,
  • ğ) compensate you for damage caused by unlawful processing.

I have no access to the information that stays on your phone, so I cannot see, correct or delete it for you; use the delete option in the app instead. You can delete your account and backup yourself in the app; for any other request about your account, write to me. If you signed in with Google, sending your request from that email address helps me find your record; if you signed in with Apple, I do not have your email, so I may ask you for more information to find it. Records at RevenueCat are anonymous, so I need to be able to find yours: include something that identifies your purchase in your request, such as the order number on the receipt Apple or Google sent you.

13. How to make a request

Send your request by email to netceapp@gmail.com. If you want to make a written request by post, ask for my postal address at the same email. Under the Turkish Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Applications to the Data Controller, a request should include your full name, your signature (for written requests), your Turkish ID number (or, for foreign nationals, your nationality and passport or ID number), an address or email address for the reply, and what you are asking for. I use these details only to handle your request.

I will answer within 30 days at the latest, free of charge. If your request is refused, you find the answer insufficient, or you get no answer within 30 days, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning the answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request (KVKK Article 14).

14. Children and teenagers

Netçe is designed for students aged 13 and over who are preparing for exams, and some of its users are under 18. That is why:

  • I never ask anyone for their name, age, school, location, photos or contact details. The account is optional; when someone signs in with Google, Google itself shares their email address, name and profile photo link, and I keep these only for sign-in.
  • I do not profile anyone, including users under 18, and I do not show ads; I never use information for marketing, sell it or share it.
  • Netçe contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries, and it does not request or use the device's advertising ID.
  • If you are under 18, talk to a parent before buying anything. Apple's Ask to Buy and Google Play's parental approval are supported: the purchase waits until a parent approves it.

Netçe is not directed at children under 13. The app asks no one for personal information and the account is optional; even if someone under 13 uses it, no contact details are collected unless they sign in with Google. If you are a parent and want a record about your child deleted (for example an account or a purchase record), write to me.

15. Security

Information on your phone is kept in the protected area the operating system gives each app. Netçe does not encrypt it separately; it relies on your phone's screen lock and encryption, so I suggest keeping your phone locked. Connections to RevenueCat and Supabase are encrypted (HTTPS). Supabase encrypts account and backup information at rest, and each account can read and write only its own records. Your sign-in session is kept on your phone, in the app's storage. Only I have access to my RevenueCat and Supabase accounts.
