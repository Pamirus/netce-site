Netçe Help

Do I need an account?

No. Netçe works without one; your study history stays on your phone. If you like, sign in with Apple or Google in Settings → Account and backup to back it up.

Will I lose my time if I close the app?

No. The timer records when it started; close the app and it picks up where it was when you open it again.

Which day does studying after midnight count for?

In Netçe the day starts at 04:00. If you study until 02:00, that time counts for the previous day.

My exam date looks wrong or missing.

Dates come from ÖSYM and MEB announcements. Dates only reported in the press are marked as such in the app and are updated once the official calendar is out. Check the final date on osym.gov.tr and meb.gov.tr. For an exam that isn't listed, enter its name and date yourself in Settings → Exam → "Other exam".

Reminders don't arrive.

Make sure notifications are allowed for Netçe in your phone's settings. You choose reminder times in Settings → Reminders, up to three.

How do I move Pro to a new phone?

Pro belongs to the App Store or Google Play account you bought it with. On the new phone, open Netçe with the same store account and tap "Restore purchases" on the Pro screen. Pro bought on the App Store doesn't carry over to Android, and Pro bought on Google Play doesn't carry over to iPhone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. The Season Pass and Lifetime are one-time payments with nothing to cancel.

Refunds

For App Store purchases, Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play purchases, use Google Play's refund process; if its window has passed, write to me.

How do I delete my data?

For everything on your phone: Settings → Privacy → "Delete all my data". If you signed in, delete your account and backup in Settings → Account and backup → "Delete account". If you can't open the app, ask for your account and backup to be deleted at netceapp@gmail.com. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

I want to report a bug.

Settings → Support → "Send feedback". Say what you did and what happened; a screenshot helps a lot.
