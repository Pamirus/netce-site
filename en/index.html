For students preparing for YKS, KPSS and other exams in Turkey

The study timer that knows your exam date

Netçe counts every minute you study by subject, shows how many days are left to your exam, and at the end of the day makes a study card you can share.

Coming soon to the App Store

  • Stopwatch and Pomodoro. Pick a subject and start; your time is kept even if the app closes.
  • Countdown. Exam calendars built in; dates only reported in the press are clearly marked.
  • Today's card. How long you studied today and how many days are left, in one image for your story or status.
  • No account needed, no ads. Your study history stays on your phone; sign in only if you want a backup.
