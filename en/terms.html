1. What Netçe is

Netçe gives students preparing for exams a study timer (stopwatch and Pomodoro), an exam countdown, study statistics and a shareable study card. It needs no account; your study history stays on your phone. If you like, you can sign in with Apple or Google to back up your history (section 7).

Netçe is not a prep school, a coaching service or an official body. It is not affiliated with ÖSYM, MEB or any other institution.

2. Exam dates

  • Exam dates in the app are for information only.
  • Every date has a source. Dates published on the website of the body that runs the exam (ÖSYM or MEB) count as official. Dates that have only been reported in the press, and are not yet on the official website, are marked in the app as unconfirmed, with a "Date as reported in the press" note.
  • Dates can change. Changes may reach the app only with an update, and with a delay.
  • For everything, including application periods and the exam's day, time and place, the official announcements of ÖSYM (osym.gov.tr) and MEB (meb.gov.tr) prevail. Check the official calendar before making important decisions.
  • With the "Other exam" option, you enter the date yourself and are responsible for it.

3. Free features and Netçe Pro

The stopwatch, Pomodoro, countdown and daily card are always free. Netçe Pro unlocks unlimited subjects, extra card themes and a card without the logo, the Sunday report and monthly statistics. The current scope of Pro is shown on the purchase screen.
